About Us

Notos Lounge Bar & Grill is located at the business centre of Juba, in Downtown Juba.

We are housed in an old stone structure, completely renovated and rehabilitated, believed to be a place where Theodore Roosevelt spent a night during his visit to Juba with the Smithsonian Expedition

We offer affordable Quality Food, Spirit and Coffee in a Lovely Ambience of Juba's historical building

  • Cuisines: Mediterranean, Indian, International, Bar and premium Steaks
  • Meals: Breakfast Lunch and After-hours
  • Diet Options: Vegetarian Friendly and Vegan options available
  • Bar & Drinks: Full Bar, Wine and Beer, Cocktails, Coffee and fresh juices
  • Seaating & Service: Indoor and Outdoor Seating, Table Service, Reservations and Takeouts

We are open daily from 09:00AM to 11:00PM. Our atmosphere combines rustic ambiance with clean, modern equipment and ethnic music background
